Mathematics

Chapter 1: Integers (पूर्णांक)

Integers are numbers that can be positive, negative, or zero.

  • Positive Integers: 1, 2, 3... (Right side of 0)
  • Negative Integers: -1, -2, -3... (Left side of 0)
  • Zero: Neither positive nor negative.

Rules of Addition:

(+) + (+) = (+)
(-) + (-) = (-)
(+) + (-) = Subtract and use the sign of the bigger number.

Science

Chapter: Nutrition in Plants (पौधों में पोषण)

Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. This process is called Photosynthesis.

Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen
  • Chlorophyll: The green pigment in leaves that captures sunlight.
  • Stomata: Tiny pores on leaves for gas exchange.

English Grammar

Parts of Speech (शब्द भेद)

Words are divided into 8 categories based on their function in a sentence.

  • Noun: Name of a person, place, or thing. (e.g., Ram, Delhi, Book)
  • Pronoun: Used in place of a noun. (e.g., He, She, It)
  • Verb: Action word. (e.g., Run, Eat, Sleep)
  • Adjective: Describes a noun. (e.g., Tall, Beautiful)
